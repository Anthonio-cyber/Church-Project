# Counsellor guide

You have been entrusted with people's confidences. This is how the platform
supports that, and what it asks of you.

---

## Before you receive anyone

**Enrol multi-factor authentication.** It is required for counsellors and
cannot be switched off. **Privacy & Security → Multi-factor authentication.**

**Set your availability.** *Counsellor Portal → Availability.* Two things
matter:

- **Current state** — available, busy, on a break, unavailable. Members see this
  when a request is being matched.
- **Maximum concurrent cases.** The platform refuses new sessions beyond it. Set
  it honestly: pastoral care done badly because you are overloaded helps nobody.

---

## Your queue

**Counsellor Portal → Client Requests.** Two sources: requests assigned to you
directly, and unassigned requests matching your approved areas and languages.

If you are not approved to work with young people, requests from minors never
appear to you at all.

**Accepting means choosing a time.** You schedule the session in the same
action, so a member never sits in an "accepted but unscheduled" limbo wondering
when someone will actually speak to them.

**Declining returns the request to the queue** for reassignment, and asks you
why. Nobody's request is dropped because one counsellor was unavailable.

---

## The waiting room

Opens fifteen minutes before the appointment. It holds one person: the member
you are meeting. There is no list of who else is waiting — no such list exists
to query.

When the member enters, you are notified. When you join, they are notified
immediately and the session opens.

---

## The session

Left: session information and the timer. Centre: the conversation. Both of you
see a standing reminder not to share passwords or financial information unless
genuinely necessary.

Counselling conversations never appear in ordinary message lists. They live in
the counselling area, with the privacy notices that belong beside them.

**Only you can close a session.** That is a pastoral record decision, not
something either party should do casually. Either of you can cancel a scheduled
session; both are told.

Sessions are not recorded. If recording is ever introduced it will require
explicit consent, an unmistakable indicator, a defined retention period and
logged access.

---

## Notes

Two kinds, and the difference matters:

**Internal pastoral notes** are your own record. The member never sees them.
They are encrypted before they reach the database, and every read is recorded —
including yours. Write what a colleague picking up the case would need, and
nothing gratuitous.

**Follow-up notes** are written deliberately for the member to read. Scripture
to sit with, an encouragement, a next step you agreed.

Choose the kind before you write. The interface makes it unmissable because
getting it wrong has consequences in both directions.

You can set a retention period on an internal note, or let the ministry's
default policy apply.

---

## Safeguarding

If someone describes danger, the platform has usually already acted: their
request was flagged, they were shown emergency and professional-help guidance
immediately, and a safeguarding lead was notified before you saw it.

If a concern emerges *during* a session, raise it with your safeguarding lead
through the ministry's procedure. Do not carry it alone, and do not decide alone
whether it counts.

**You cannot intervene physically, and neither can this platform.** Where
someone is in immediate danger, the answer is local emergency services. Say so
plainly. Pastoral care walks alongside professional help; it does not replace
it, and implying otherwise does real harm.

---

## What members can and cannot see

They see: your display name, ministry role, biography, areas of service,
languages, availability, and any follow-up notes you wrote for them.

They do not see: your internal notes, your other cases, or anything about anyone
else.

---

## What administrators can and cannot see

Counselling administrators see the operational shape of the queue — assignment,
scheduling, capacity. They do not see your conversations or your notes. The
operations API does not even select what the member wishes to discuss.

A safeguarding lead may reach counselling records where there is a safeguarding
concern, with a written reason, and that access is recorded permanently against
the record.

Administrative rank alone opens nothing.
